Southern Charmers

      I’m thankful for the way you move;
      It’s dancing through the world
      Like raindrops having naught to prove,
      Like sunbeam’s glow unfurled

      I’m thankful for the sights you see,
      The daybreak from the peak 
      And that you share those sights with me
      The waterfall and creek

      I’m thankful for the books you read
      With tortured villains, true,
      But also heroes of great deed —
      Jacques’s mice live on in you

      I’m thankful for the zeal you live
      It’s not just something had
      But part of you, a gift you give
      To those with caution clad

      I’m thankful for your presence here
      Not just “here,” texting me
      But for your beauty in the world
      A light, for those who see
